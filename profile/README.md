## 🕯️ The studio

Franord Games is a small independent studio built around a simple idea: **games should leave a mark long after the screen goes dark.**

With roots stretching from Schleswig-Holstein to Upper Franconia, we bring different landscapes and perspectives together to create worlds with their own atmosphere, identity, and secrets.

We are at the beginning of the journey, building our craft one system, one creature, and one late-night idea at a time.

### Aether & Iron

*A world where the fog breathes, machines whisper, and survival is never guaranteed.*

</div>

**Hollowbloom** is our dark-fantasy strategy and survival project, set in an arcane industrial age. Here, machinery runs on Aether instead of coal, the mist carries a will of its own, and every expedition risks becoming the last.

- **Genre:** Strategy · Roguelike · Survival
- **Visual direction:** Dark steampunk, hand-crafted 2D art, hex-grid battlefields
- **Core ideas:** Tactical choices, dangerous exploration, terrain resonance, and dwindling light
- **Engine:** Godot 4 with C#

> *When the lantern fades, the world begins to move.*

*Hollowbloom is in development. Features and design may change as the project evolves.*

## 🛠️ How we build

We care about the feeling of a world as much as the systems beneath it.

- **Atmosphere first.** Every environment, sound, and silhouette should tell part of the story.
- **Choices with consequences.** Strategy should reward curiosity, planning, and adaptation.
- **Hand-crafted identity.** We want our games to feel made by people, not assembled from templates.
- **Small team, ambitious worlds.** Build carefully, learn constantly, and keep moving forward.
